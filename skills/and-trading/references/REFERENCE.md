# PINTAR Trading Reference

## Crypto
- DEX: Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Raydium, Jupiter
- CEX: Binance, Coinbase, Bybit, OKX, Kraken
- DeFi: Aave, Compound, Lido, EigenLayer
- Smart Contracts: ERC-20, ERC-721, Solidity, Rust (Solana)
- APIs: CCXT (unified), web3.py, ethers.js
- Indicators: RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, EMA/SMA, Volume Profile

## Stocks
- Brokers: Interactive Brokers, Alpaca, TD Ameritrade
- APIs: yfinance, alpha_vantage, polygon.io, finnhub
- Analysis: P/E ratio, EPS, DCF, market cap, dividend yield
- Patterns: Head & Shoulders, Double Top/Bottom, Cup & Handle
- Indices: S&P 500, NASDAQ, Dow Jones, FTSE, Nikkei

## Forex
- Major: EUR/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/USD, USD/CHF
- Indicators: CPI, GDP, NFP, interest rates, PMI
- Sessions: Asian, London, New York overlap

## Risk Management
- Position sizing: 1-2% risk per trade
- Stop-loss: always defined before entry
- Risk/Reward: minimum 1:2 ratio
- Max drawdown: 20-25% portfolio

## Backtesting
- Python: backtrader, vectorbt, zipline-reloaded
- Walk-forward optimization, Monte Carlo simulation
- Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, max drawdown
